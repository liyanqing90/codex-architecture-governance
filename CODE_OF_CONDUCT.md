# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors, maintainers, and users commit to a harassment-free community
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private, sensitive, and security-related information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers to disclose vulnerability details before remediation.
- Using architecture review as a pretext to shame contributors or teams.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when
behavior threatens a safe and constructive community.

Maintainers must handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and explain enforcement decisions to affected parties when it is safe to do so.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.
