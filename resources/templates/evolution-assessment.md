# Technology evolution assessment

Bind this report and the corresponding structured fields in the Architecture
Decision. Record each measurement, official-source capture, and pilot result in
a project-relative evidence file with its exact SHA-256; status text alone is
not adoption evidence.

## Current baseline

Record the current owner, observed measures, local-correction option, and the
consequence of making no architecture or technology change.

## Measurable gap

Record the quality scenario, current value, target, measurement method,
evidence, and adoption threshold.

## Volatile claims

For each version, support, compatibility, security, license, price, limit,
roadmap, or benchmark claim, record the official publisher, URL, exact scope,
access date, and freshness conclusion.

## Compatibility and migration

Record affected consumers, public and persisted contracts, data movement,
mixed-version behavior, migration slices, duration, and cost.

## Operational and team fit

Record the accountable owner, required skills, support and on-call model,
observability, failure semantics, security implications, and operating cost.

## Lock-in and exit

Record proprietary surfaces, portability limits, exit cost, and data recovery
semantics.

## Rollback

Record the rollback point, irreversible gate, validation method, and the state
that must remain compatible until acceptance.

## Shadow or pilot evidence

Record the bounded cohort or shadow path, evidence owner, success and stop
criteria, observed measures, and whether the pilot completed.

## Revisit triggers

Record measurable metrics or events, thresholds, owner, review date or cadence,
and the evidence needed to reopen the decision.
